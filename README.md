## `> whoami`

I work at the intersection of **software engineering, AI automation, business operations, and product leadership**.

My background in **HR, operations, and IT** influences how I build: understand the people, map the workflow, question the assumptions, and then engineer the solution.

I care more about **reliable systems and real outcomes** than impressive-looking tech stacks.
